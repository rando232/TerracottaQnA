## Color Comparison

### Before (First Terracotta Attempt)
- Too muted/brown
- Not enough vibrancy
- Primary color: #a18072 (dull brown)

### After (Brighter Update)
- Much more vibrant orange/coral tones
- Better readability and contrast
- Primary color: #d4582f (bright burnt orange)
- Backgrounds: Warm cream/ivory instead of stark white

## Visual Impact

## Design Philosophy

The updated palette reflects:
- **Ancient Terracotta Pottery**: Bright, warm clay colors
- **Mediterranean Warmth**: Sun-baked terracotta tones
- **Modern Accessibility**: High contrast for readability
- **Energy & Approachability**: Vibrant oranges instead of muddy browns

## Specific Changes

### Backgrounds
- Main page: `bg-slate-50` → `bg-amber-50` (warm cream)
- All cards: `bg-white` → `bg-orange-50` (soft ivory)

### Terracotta Scale
- Primary (600): #a18072 → **#d4582f** (much brighter!)

- Text on bg-orange-50: >7:1 contrast
- Terracotta-600 buttons: High visibility
- Focus indicators: Clear and distinct
